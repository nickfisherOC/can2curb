Local & founder-led

A neighbourly service, built around getting bin day right.

Can 2 Curb is an independent Edmonton bin valet service. We move your bins to the curb before collection and bring them back after — reliably, respectfully, and on your schedule. Here's who we are and what we stand for.

Meet the founder

Hello — I'm Doug.

Can 2 Curb started with a simple idea: getting your bins to the curb and back should be one less thing to think about. I built this service for my Edmonton neighbours — for busy households, for anyone who finds heavy bins a struggle, and for property owners who just want collection day handled properly.

What we stand for

The values behind every bin day.

We're a new local business, so these are the commitments we're building Can 2 Curb around — the standards we want every customer to feel.

Reliability

Collection day runs on a schedule, so we do too. Once your service is set, showing up on time is our job to remember — not yours.

Respect

Your property is yours. We treat every driveway, gate, and quiet street the way we'd want ours treated — courteous and unobtrusive.

Clear communication

Straightforward answers, no jargon, and an easy way to reach a real local person when your plans or schedule change.

Care for your property

Careful handling and tidy placement. Bins go out neatly and come back to the exact spot you asked for — gates closed behind us.

Local accountability

We live and work in Edmonton. That means we answer for our own service — no call centre, no runaround, just a neighbour you can hold to it.

How we work

Our service standards.

These are the commitments we hold ourselves to on every visit. They describe how we intend to operate — the formal policies below are being finalized before launch.

  • Careful handling of your property

    We move bins with care and leave your driveway, walkways, and gates as we found them.

  • We follow your access instructions

    Gate codes, side yards, long driveways, and where bins live — we do it your way, every time.

  • Dependable scheduling

    Bins out before collection, returned after. We track Edmonton's changing collection days so you don't have to.
