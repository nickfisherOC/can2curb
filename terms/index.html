About our service

Can 2 Curb is a bin valet service. Before your collection day, we move your designated bins from their usual location to the curb, and after collection we return them to their spot.

Can 2 Curb is not a garbage collection company. We do not collect, empty, haul, or dispose of waste. The City of Edmonton performs all waste, recycling, food scraps, and yard-waste collection. Our service is limited to moving your bins between your property and the curb.
