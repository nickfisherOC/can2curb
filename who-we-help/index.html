Who we help

Built for the people who would rather not think about bin day.

Everyone has a different reason for wanting bin day handled — a bad knee, a busy calendar, a rental across town, or a whole portfolio of properties. Whatever yours is, Can 2 Curb takes the trip to the curb and back off your plate.

Seniors

Stay in the home you love, without the weekly haul.

Wheeling heavy bins down an icy driveway is one of those chores that quietly gets harder every year. Can 2 Curb handles that trip so staying in your own home stays comfortable — and so family across town can worry about it a little less.

  • No heavy lifting on bin day

    We move the bins so you don't have to.

  • Steadier footing in winter

    One less reason to head out onto an icy path.

  • Set it once

    A recurring routine you never have to remember.

Family caregivers

One less thing to drive across the city for.

When you are helping a parent or relative stay independent, the little tasks add up — and bin day always seems to land on your busiest morning. Setting up recurring service means a parent's bins are handled without another trip on your calendar.

  • Manage it from anywhere

    Arrange service for a loved one's address, even if you live elsewhere.

  • Nobody needs to be home

    We follow the access instructions you set once.

  • Reliable routine

    Steady, predictable service you can count on each cycle.

People with limited mobility

A practical hand with an awkward, heavy job.

Full bins are bulky and hard to steer, and a long or sloped driveway makes it worse. If lifting, pushing, or balancing on uneven ground is difficult, Can 2 Curb quietly takes that task on so bin day is no longer something to plan around.

  • We do the pushing and lifting

    No wrestling bins over gravel, snow, or a slope.

  • Long & steep driveways welcome

    Exactly the situations we are built for.

  • Bins back where you can reach them

    Returned to the exact spot that works for you.

Group homes

A dependable routine your staff can rely on.

In a supported-living or group home, waste and recycling days are one more thing on an already full plate for staff. A standing bin service turns collection day into something that just happens — consistent, hands-off, and one fewer task during a shift.

  • Consistent every cycle

    The same reliable routine, regardless of who is on shift.

  • Handles multiple bins

    Larger households often mean more bins — we plan for that.

  • One point of contact

    Easy to set up and easy to reach when something changes.
