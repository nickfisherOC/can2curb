Overview

Can 2 Curb ("Can 2 Curb", "we", "us", or "our") is a local bin valet service operating in Edmonton, Alberta. We respect your privacy and want you to understand what personal information we collect, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

This policy applies to information collected through our website and when you contact us about our service. By using our website or submitting a form, you agree to the practices described in this policy. Legal entity name TBD

Information we collect

We only collect the information we need to answer your questions and provide our service. When you fill out a form or contact us, we may collect:

  • Your name — so we know who we are speaking with.
  • Email address — to reply to you and send service-related messages.
  • Phone number — if you provide it, so we can reach you about your request.
  • Service address — to confirm whether we serve your area and to provide the service.
  • Property details — such as where your bins are stored, access instructions, number of bins, and your collection schedule.
  • Your message — anything else you choose to tell us in a form or email.

You do not have to give us this information, but we may not be able to answer your request or provide service without it.

How we use your information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Confirm whether your address is within our service area;
  • Respond to your questions and quote requests;
  • Set up, schedule, and provide our bin valet service;
  • Contact you about your service, including changes and reminders;
  • Keep basic records of our customers and requests; and
  • Improve our service and website.

We do not use your information for unrelated purposes, and we do not sell it. See "Sharing your information" below.

Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We only share it in limited situations:

  • With service providers who help us run our website, forms, or business, and only as needed to do that work;
  • When required by law, or to protect our rights, safety, or property; and
  • With your consent, or at your direction.

We do not share your information with advertisers. Service-provider list TBD

Your choices and rights

You have choices about your personal information. Subject to applicable law, you may:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you;
  • Correct information that is wrong or out of date;
  • Ask us to delete your information, where we are able to; and
  • Withdraw consent or ask us to stop contacting you.

To make any of these requests, email us at hello@can2curb.ca and we will respond within a reasonable time. We may need to confirm your identity before acting on a request.

Data retention

We keep your personal information only as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy, to provide our service, and to meet legal or business record-keeping needs. After that, we take steps to delete or de-identify it.

We have not yet set specific retention periods. Retention periods TBD

Children's privacy

Our service and website are intended for adults arranging service for their property. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps to remove it.
