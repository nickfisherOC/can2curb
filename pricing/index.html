Pricing questions

Good to know about our plans.

Curbside ($49/month) takes your bins to the curb before your City of Edmonton collection and returns them the next day, on a recurring schedule. Curbside + Clean ($69/month) includes everything in Curbside, plus a full bin clean and sanitise once a month. Excess Trash Removal (from $80) is a one-time haul-away for extra bags and items that won't fit your bins.
