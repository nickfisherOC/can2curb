Local & accountable

A local service built around reliability.

Can 2 Curb was created to solve a simple but recurring problem for Edmonton residents: getting bins to the curb safely, consistently, and on time. We believe a local service should be easy to reach, clear about what it does, and dependable when customers need it.

Founder Doug has spent the last decade running a property-management company across Edmonton — looking after group homes, youth homes, and residential properties. He kept running into the same small problem: bins forgotten, left at the curb all day, blocking parking. Can 2 Curb is his answer to it.
